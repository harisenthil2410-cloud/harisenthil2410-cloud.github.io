## What I'm learning toward

My coursework and projects both track the same line: getting a sensor's signal off a breadboard, through a microcontroller, and into a decision — reliably, in real time, and without babysitting it. That's meant going deep on three things in parallel:

- **Embedded firmware** — 8051 and ARM Cortex‑M33 (Renesas RA6E2), interrupts, timers, RTC, UART, I2C, and peripheral configuration through both bare‑metal register work and vendor tooling like Renesas's FSP.
- **Wireless & edge networks** — ESP32 and LoRaWAN for multi-zone sensor transmission, plus the protocol side of high-speed interfaces like ONFI for NAND flash.
- **Applied ML for anomaly detection** — using models like Isolation Forest on live sensor streams to flag anomalies rather than just threshold-check them.
